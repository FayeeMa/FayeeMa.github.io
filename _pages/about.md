I’m a PhD student in Statistics and Data Science at the [Wharton School](https://www.wharton.upenn.edu/) of the [University of Pennsylvania](https://www.upenn.edu/), advised by Prof. [Ying Jin](https://ying531.github.io/). I hold a bachelor’s degree in Mathematics and Physics from [Tsinghua University](https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/).

My current research focuses on conformal prediction. Broadly, I’m interested in how statistical methods can help us quantify uncertainty, uncover scientific insights, and accelerate discovery. My earlier research focused on statistical network analysis, including graphon estimation, latent space models, and generative models for structured data.

I began my research journey working on graphon models with Prof. [Weichi Wu](https://www.stat.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/info/1023/1048.htm) and Prof. [Chenlei Leng](https://warwick.ac.uk/fac/sci/statistics/staff/academic-research/leng/). I also spent a wonderful summer at the [University of Michigan](https://lsa.umich.edu/stats), working with Prof. [Ji Zhu](https://dept.stat.lsa.umich.edu/~jizhu/) and Prof. [Gongjun Xu](https://sites.google.com/umich.edu/gongjunxu) on generative models for attributed hypergraphs. I’m grateful to them for their mentorship, and to [Xinyuan Fan](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=i6lqKFoAAAAJ&hl=en) and [Shihao Wu](https://sites.google.com/umich.edu/shihaowu/) for their generous guidance and insightful discussions.
